The Earth Metabolome Initiative is taking shape as an open, collaborative
effort to document the world’s chemodiversity.

- We have started gathering a consortium of supporting researchers. Meet the
  growing {{< link members />}} network.
- We are applying to funding opportunities.
- We are developing the [Digital Botanical Gardens Initiative](https://www.dbgi.org/)
  as a pilot for EMI, with progress documented in the
  [Open DBGI Notebook](https://www.dbgi.org/dendron-dbgi/).

More soon.
