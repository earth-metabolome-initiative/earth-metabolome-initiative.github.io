The ViNEsh BioBlitz is a place-based EMI mission in Valais, Switzerland. It
provides a focused setting for bringing field observations, biological samples,
environmental context, and chemical diversity together.

The mission page can grow into a living field record: its objectives, dates,
partners, sampling sites, protocols, participants, and openly released outputs
can be added as they are confirmed.

The map currently indicates Valais broadly and does not represent a precise
sampling site or project boundary.
